# Koot photography brief

The catalogue photos work, but they are only about 750 px wide, so they look soft when shown large. These prompts recreate the same look at high resolution and add the shots the site is missing. Ask for **at least 3000 px on the long side**, and save as JPG or PNG.

## Shared style (paste at the start of every prompt)

> Photorealistic professional food photography, not an illustration. Warm natural daylight from the upper left with soft dappled leaf shadows. Beige stone tabletop, an olive-green linen napkin, handmade cream ceramic bowls and plates. Camera at about 45 degrees, shallow depth of field, rich natural colour, appetizing and fresh. No text, no logos, no packaging, no people, no hands, no cartoon elements.

Keep the same table, napkin, bowls and light in every shot so the whole site looks like one photo shoot.

## Product packs (not a photo prompt)

The pack photos you already have are good, but they sit on a white background. Please also send each pack as a **PNG with a transparent background**, at the same angle and lighting. That lets us place packs on any background without a white box around them.
